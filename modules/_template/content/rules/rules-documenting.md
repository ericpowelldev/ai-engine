Structure rules govern how a doc is organized; writing rules govern how it reads.

Example entries (replace with your own):

## documenting-structure-lead-with-the-point

The first paragraph answers "what is this and why do I care"; background follows the point, never precedes it.

## documenting-writing-present-tense-intent

Docs describe current intent in present tense: no changelogs of how the doc evolved, no "we decided" narration. Readers need the state, not the meeting.
