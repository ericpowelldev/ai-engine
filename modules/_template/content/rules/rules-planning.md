# Planning Rules

<!-- Rules the agent loads before creating, editing, or executing a plan. -->

Example entries (replace with your own):

## planning-define-done

Every plan states its finish line: what must be true, and how each part of it is checked, for the work to count as done.

## planning-name-the-risks

Every plan states what could invalidate it (an unverified assumption, an external dependency) next to the step that depends on it, not in a risks appendix nobody reads.
